A contribution graph for a whole life

What a year of little squares taught me about consistency.

GitHub has been drawing a picture of my work for years — one green square at a time. At some point I looked at it and wondered why the rest of my life didn't get the same treatment. The workouts, the Korean flashcards, the piano practice, the books: all of it real work, none of it counted.

So I started counting. Five categories, one color each, one square per day. The rules are simple: did I show up, and roughly how hard? That's it. No streaks-or-die guilt, no gamified nonsense — just an honest record.

LANGUAGE

The surprising part isn't the motivation — it's the honesty. A blank week of Fitness orange doesn't lie to you the way memory does. And a full row of Language green in a hard month is proof you kept a promise nobody was watching.

This site is that record, made public. Learning in public, scored in public.
